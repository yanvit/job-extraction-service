We’re seeking a Senior Mobile Software Engineer (React Native) to building and scaling mobile applications for a Retail Brokerage business. If you enjoy working on production-ready mobile apps and want to make a real impact on high-traffic financial platforms — we’d love to hear from you.

## Core Competence

- Proven senior-level experience in React Native development for commercial mobile applications.
- Strong knowledge of TypeScript and modern JavaScript (ES6+).
- Hands-on experience with Redux for state management and RxJS for reactive programming.
- Solid understanding of mobile app architecture, performance optimization, and best practices.
- Experience setting up and delivering mobile applications from scratch to production.
- Ability to collaborate closely with a web-focused team and integrate mobile solutions with existing BSG sites.
- Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive, ownership-driven mindset.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working in FinTech / Retail Brokerage or other high-reliability domains.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines for mobile applications.
- Experience working in cross-functional teams (design, backend, product).
- Upper-intermediate or higher level of English for effective communication in an international environment.

## What We Offer You

- Impactful Product: Direct involvement in building a mobile app for BSG brokerage platforms used by real customers.
- Professional Team: Collaboration with an experienced Web team in a structured corporate environment.
- Flexible Setup: Contract-based cooperation with a clear scope and timeline.
