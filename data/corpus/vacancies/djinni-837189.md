# DevOps Engineer (Hetzner Cloud)

OMNICORE

$$$$

Продуктова компанія

OMNICORE is an international product and service company specializing in e-commerce. We create, develop, and manage online businesses for leading retail players in the fashion industry, with a focus on clothing, footwear, and accessories.

We combine consulting, design, web development, content production, marketing, customer service, and operational management to deliver comprehensive and effective omnichannel e-commerce solutions.
For over nine years, we have been helping our partners grow their online businesses. Our clients include ua.puma.com, adidas.ua, adidas.kz, adidas.kg, the marketplaces md-fashion.ua, ultra-shop.com, frgroup.kz, frgroup.uz, and others.

Since 2022, the company has expanded its operations to the Republic of Kazakhstan. We provide a full range of services, including eCommerce-as-a-Service (EaaS) and 3PL solutions, becoming a regional fulfillment hub.

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to strengthen our team.

## Requirements

- At least 3 years of experience as a DevOps Engineer / SRE;
- English – strong Intermediate or higher;
- Experience with HA and HL would be a plus;
- OS: Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Alpine);
- Application support: PHP (required), JS;
- Infrastructure: Ansible, Terraform, Docker;
- Cloud providers: Hetzner Cloud (strongly required!), GCP;
- Databases:- MySQL (standalone, master-slave, Percona cluster)
- PostgreSQL (standalone, master-slave, Patroni cluster)
- MongoDB (standalone, replicaset)
- Elasticsearch (standalone, cluster)
- Redis (standalone, Sentinel, cluster)

- HTTP/Proxy/LB: NGINX, HAProxy, Varnish, Cloud-managed load balancers;
- CI/CD: GitOps, Bitbucket Pipelines, SonarQube, Dependency-Track;
- Programming: Python, Bash scripting (PHP is a plus);
- Observability: ELK / OpenSearch stack, Prometheus / Grafana stack, Alerts management;
- InfoSec/Security: DevSecOps principles, Vault, Teleport.
