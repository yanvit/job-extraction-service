# Senior Golang Backend Developer

Hanna Robulets Company

$$$$

Senior Golang Backend Developer

Location: Remote
Workload: Full-time
Project: Long-term

About the role
We’re looking for a Senior Golang Backend Developer to build and scale microservices-based systems and help the engineering team migrate existing backend services from Node.js/NestJS to Go.
The role involves designing high-load services, working with distributed systems, improving backend architecture, and implementing modern patterns for asynchronous processing and real-time communication.
You’ll work with an existing production codebase, contribute to the migration strategy, and help the team gradually transition services from Node.js to a scalable Go-based architecture.

Requirements
- 4+ years of backend development experience
- 3+ years of hands-on experience with Go / Golang
- Strong understanding of Go fundamentals, concurrency, goroutines, channels, interfaces, contexts, and error handling
- Strong experience with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis
- Solid understanding of microservices architecture
- Experience building high-load and distributed systems
- Experience with asynchronous processing, queues, and event-driven architectures
- Experience building REST APIs, WebSockets (WS/WSS), and RPC/gRPC services
- Ability to design scalable, maintainable, and reliable backend services
- Ability to read, understand, refactor, and migrate existing codebases
- Strong understanding of performance optimization and observability
- High attention to detail

Nice to have
- Experience with Node.js / NestJS, especially in projects migrating from Node.js to Go
- Previous experience migrating production services between technology stacks
- Deep knowledge of Go concurrency patterns and performance optimization
- Experience with gRPC / Protocol Buffers
- Deep knowledge of Redis, including MULTI/EXEC, data structures, Lua scripts, and Pub/Sub
- Experience with message brokers and event-driven systems
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud environments
- Basic understanding of prompt engineering, AI agents, and AI skills

Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain backend services using Golang
- Design and implement high-load, scalable microservices
- Help the engineering team migrate existing Node.js/NestJS services to Go
- Analyze the existing Node.js architecture and define appropriate Go implementations
- Refactor and gradually replace existing Node.js services while maintaining system stability
- Design APIs and service-to-service communication using REST, WebSockets, RPC/gRPC, and event-driven patterns
- Work with asynchronous architectures, queues, and distributed systems
- Optimize backend performance, reliability, and scalability
- Improve architecture and engineering practices during the migration
- Review code and contribute to technical decisions
- Collaborate with developers and other cross-functional team members
