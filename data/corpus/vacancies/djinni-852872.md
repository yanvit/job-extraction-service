⚾️ Duration: long-term

Responsibilities
Develop and maintain backend services in Rust for lending and repayment logic.

Build accounting-grade data storage with a full audit trail.
Match blockchain activity against internal and banking records.

Must have
Hands-on blockchain experience (critical): commercial experience building backend systems that interact with Ethereum or other EVM chains, including RPC, event indexing, transaction lifecycle, signing and smart-contract integration.

7+ years in commercial software development, including at least 2–3 years of Rust in production.
Async Rust, concurrency, and a solid understanding of how distributed systems fail.
Strong PostgreSQL: data modelling, migrations, transactional integrity.
Experience with financial systems where precision matters: payments, ledgers, billing, accounting.
Strong testing culture.
Good written English, including the ability to explain technical decisions clearly.

Nice to have
Solidity: reading and reviewing smart contracts.
Account abstraction / smart wallets.
Key management and signing infrastructure.
TypeScript / React basics.
Infrastructure as code, self-hosted environments.
Background in lending, payments or payroll.
