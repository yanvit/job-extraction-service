# React Native Developer

Come Back Agency

$

Come Back Agency helps US software and technology companies run their hiring process. We work with delivery and leadership teams to define roles, screen candidates, and manage interviews. Successful candidates are hired directly by the company and become part of its team.

This position is with a US-based software company providing custom development and AI implementation for North American clients. The team builds and maintains production software and works in long-term client engagements. Team members operate as part of an internal, distributed team and collaborate directly with client stakeholders.

About the Role
We are looking for a React Native Developer with 1.5-2+ years of commercial experience to join our mobile development team. You will build, maintain, and improve cross-platform mobile applications using React Native and Expo. You will work closely with the Head of React Native and communicate directly with US-based clients.

The ideal candidate has hands-on experience developing mobile applications, can independently handle tasks within their level of expertise, and knows how to troubleshoot mobile-specific issues and optimize application performance.

What You Will Do
- Build and maintain cross-platform mobile applications using React Native, Expo, and TypeScript.
- Implement new features, integrate REST APIs and third-party services, and maintain existing code.
- Develop responsive interfaces and ensure a consistent user experience across iOS and Android.
- Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks, improve application stability, and maintain code quality.
- Investigate platform-specific issues, handle permissions, and troubleshoot native-related problems and build failures.
- Work with Expo EAS Build and support application builds and releases.
- Participate in code reviews, discuss technical decisions, and collaborate with the Head of React Native.
- Communicate directly with US-based clients to clarify requirements, discuss implementation details, and provide updates on development tasks.

Requirements
- 1.5–2+ years of commercial experience in React Native development, preferably with Expo.
- Strong knowledge of React Hooks, component lifecycle, and TypeScript.
- Hands-on experience with React Navigation and/or Expo Router.
- Understanding of iOS and Android differences, Platform.OS, and permission handling.
- Experience integrating REST APIs and working with asynchronous data.
- Understanding of state management approaches and experience with AsyncStorage.
- Experience with Expo EAS Build and familiarity with App Store Connect and Google Play Console.
- Basic understanding of CI/CD pipelines, Git, and mobile app versioning, including buildNumber and versionCode.
- Basic understanding of Bridge, JSI, and Fabric and their purpose.
- Upper-Intermediate English (B2) or higher for direct written and verbal communication with US-based clients.
- Ability to work independently on assigned tasks, troubleshoot technical issues, and explain technical decisions.

Nice to Have
- Experience with react-native-reanimated and react-native-gesture-handler.
- Knowledge of UI optimization and performance profiling.
- Hands-on experience implementing push notifications using expo-notifications.
- Experience integrating authentication providers such as Google, Apple, and Facebook.
- Experience with Firebase Analytics or similar analytics tools.
- Experience working with GraphQL APIs.
- At least one mobile application released to production through the App Store or Google Play.

What We Offer
- Fully remote, full-time cooperation
- Stable working hours aligned with the US market: 16:00–00:00 Kyiv time
- Paid vacation and sick days
- English lessons and speaking clubs
- No time-tracking software
- Opportunities for professional growth and increased ownership within the team
- Compensation reviews based on performance

Hiring Stages: Recruiter Screening → Technical Interview з Head of Development + Live Coding (60хв)

Apply with your resume and a short note outlining your relevant React Native experience. You can also submit your application through our website at comeback.ua. Selected candidates will be contacted by Come Back Agency.
