# Senior iOS Engineer

Oxigen

$$$

Our client is a fast-moving US-market startup with a live iOS gaming product and a growing user base. The engineering team is lean, moves fast, and takes pride in the quality of what they ship. Product, design, and engineering work closely together – no hand-offs, no silos.

They're looking for a Senior iOS Engineer who's built consumer apps that real people use. Someone who knows what makes an app feel right, catches performance issues before users do, and can drive technical decisions without waiting to be told what to do.

## What you'll work on

- Implement new product features end-to-end in Swift
- Work closely with game designers and backend engineers on gameplay flows
- Take ownership of performance, memory, and responsiveness on your features
- Review code and actively push iOS quality standards across the codebase
- Use AI coding tools in your daily workflow to move faster and write less boilerplate
- Weigh in on architecture and tooling decisions

## What we're looking for

- 3+ years of iOS development on consumer apps shipped to the App Store
- Gaming industry experience is a must – or at minimum, a high-engagement B2C entertainment app (social, streaming, lifestyle)
- Solid Swift fundamentals; strong grasp of UIKit and/or SwiftUI
- Hands-on experience with performance profiling and optimization
- Used to working async with distributed teams
- AI tools are part of your daily routine, not an afterthought
- B2+ English

Необхідні навички

Swift, iOS development, UIKit, SwiftUI
