# Senior Android Developer

BillEase

$$$$

Продуктова компанія

Join the senior team behind the Billease Android app — a Kotlin & Jetpack Compose fintech product that millions rely on for payments, credit, and secure account access. You'll own features end-to-end, raise the engineering bar alongside other senior developers, and help keep a fintech-grade codebase fast, secure, and a pleasure to work in.

What you'll do
- Own features end-to-end — from the networking and data layers through to pixel-accurate Jetpack Compose UI, verified in production.
- Help uphold and evolve the architecture: a clean, layered flow (data → domain → presentation), clear module boundaries, and reactive single-source-of-truth state.
- Raise the bar through rigorous code review, shared standards, and mentoring — contributing to a strong senior team, not working in isolation.
- Keep the app crash-resilient, performant, and secure — careful recomposition, disciplined null-safety, and sensitive data encrypted at rest.
- Investigate and drive production issues to root cause, then ship the fix and the regression guard.
- Help shape conventions, module design, and technical direction together with the team as the product scales.

What we're looking for
These are the bar for the role — a strong senior should meet essentially all of them, with real depth (not just exposure) in the core engineering areas.
- 7+ years of professional Android development, including meaningful time in a senior or lead role owning architecture and technical decisions — not just closing tickets.
- Expert-level Kotlin — structured concurrency with coroutines, Flow / StateFlow, strict null-safety, and a feel for clean, stable public API design.
- Deep production Jetpack Compose: state hoisting, recomposition and performance tuning, custom layouts, and type-safe Compose Navigation at scale.
- Command of MVVM / clean architecture and DI (Hilt/Dagger) — able to shape and uphold layering and patterns across a large codebase, not just follow them.
- Fluent in a multi-module Gradle codebase and a disciplined Git / MR-based workflow with thorough reviews.
- Solid REST networking (Ktor/Retrofit) and JSON serialization, local persistence (Room), and a proven security-first instinct for auth, sessions, and data at rest.
- Fluent working from Figma — reading designs, specs, and a design-token system, and turning them into faithful, reusable Compose components in close partnership with designers.
- Effective, judgment-driven use of AI coding tools (e.g. Claude Code, Copilot) — using them to move faster while critically reviewing and validating everything they produce to a senior's standard.

The way you show up
- Actively drive technical and feature discussions — asking sharp questions, weighing trade-offs, and helping the team land on the right approach.
- Lead the charge when it counts: investigate and triage production issues, trace root causes, and drive them to resolution.
- Take ownership end-to-end — you plan before you build, flag risks early, and see work through to verified-in-production.
- Collaborate well in a cross-functional, fast-moving team and are comfortable with ambiguity.

Nice to have
- Fintech or payments experience at scale — with a security-first mindset.
- Hands-on with data-at-rest encryption (Keystore / Tink), auth/session handling, biometrics, or KYC / liveness flows.
- Feature-flagged delivery, analytics instrumentation, and crash monitoring.
- Static analysis and quality gates (detekt / ktlint) and E2E UI testing.
- Experience migrating legacy XML/Fragment screens to Compose, and contributing to a shared design system.
