# Team Lead Android

RedCore

🔥

$$$$

Продуктова компанія

RedCore is an international business group that creates technological solutions for digital markets. Our products and services cover fintech, marketing, e-commerce, customer service, communications and regulatory technologies.

We are looking for a Team Lead Android to join our team!

## Requirements

- 5+ years of commercial Android development experience.
- Strong expertise in Kotlin and the Android SDK.
- Experience as a Team Lead with substantial people management: task breakdown, workload balancing, delivery tracking, unblocking, mentoring, and technical feedback.
- Experience running code reviews and setting up efficient, reliable review processes.
- Strong understanding of HTTP/API and client-server communication.
- Mandatory experience in traffic arbitrage with Android and/or iOS applications.
- Solid understanding of the development, support, and lifecycle of arbitrage mobile apps.
- Practical experience with AppsFlyer, Firebase, OneSignal, attribution, deep linking, push notifications, remote config, and other third-party mobile services.
- Ability to quickly onboard onto existing codebases and work effectively with unfamiliar code.
- Flexibility in finding alternative technical approaches for gray-area application logic, especially when standard solutions are not viable.
- Hands-on experience using AI Code Agents (Cursor, Codex, etc.) in development, code review, or engineering workflows.

## Will be plus

- Cross-platform mobile experience.
- Understanding of native vs cross-platform approaches, including their trade-offs.
- Backend and/or frontend development experience.
- Familiarity with trackers and TDS platforms (Keitaro or similar).
- Hands-on work with Google Play Console, including publishing, signing, review, and updates.
- Experience building or supporting WebView-based applications.
- Practical experience setting up and maintaining CI/CD.
- Exposure to analytics and anti-fraud solutions.

## Soft Skills

- High sense of ownership and independence.
- Excellent team collaboration skills.
- Ability to deliver results without constant top-down control.

## Responsibilities

Technical Leadership, Ownership & Support

- Own the team’s technical execution across a high volume of Android apps.
- Stay hands-on for complex, unfamiliar, or non-standard tasks, and help developers unblock on hard technical questions.
- Keep engineering pragmatic: simple, working solutions that are fast to ship and easy to support.
- Bring AI into development and review workflows to speed up delivery.
- Partner with the Tech Lead on technical decisions and project development, and bring in new approaches and technologies that help the team deliver more with less effort.
Planning & Delivery Coordination

- Organize daily work: break down tasks, assign ownership, balance workload, track deadlines, remove blockers, and move apps through development, testing, and release.
- Streamline development and code review, including AI workflows, to keep cycle times short.
- Align with Product, QA, and other stakeholders on scope, priorities, and timelines.
People Leadership & Development

- Lead the existing Android team through 1:1s, mentoring, and day-to-day guidance.
- Understand each engineer’s strengths and growth areas, and help them become more independent and effective.
Communication & Reporting

- Act as the coordination point between Android developers and the rest of the team, keeping stakeholders informed on progress, risks, and delivery status.
- Stay aligned with the Tech Lead and the team on the same technical language, and evaluate developers’ solutions on technical grounds.
