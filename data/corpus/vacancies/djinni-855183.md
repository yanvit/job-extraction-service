About the Role
We're looking for a curious and motivated Junior С# Engineer to join our team. You'll be building backend services, REST APIs, with some exposure to cloud infrastructure along the way.

We work in pairs — juniors are always paired with someone at a similar or slightly higher level, not just for code reviews, but for day-to-day coding, architecture decisions, and problem solving. This is production work: you'll ship backend services that customers rely on, with the safety net of another teammate beside you.

The client is US-based, so the team works US business hours; for engineers in Ukraine, this means your working day ends around midnight Kyiv time. This role can be full-time or part-time, depending on your availability.

What You'll Work On
- Backend Services & APIs — build and maintain REST APIs and backend services for client platforms.
- Azure and Cloudflare infrastructure — Azure DevOps pipelines for CI/CD
- Salesforce backend for data storage

Tech Stack
- Backend — C# (our main language), REST API.
- Cloud — Azure - nice-to-have, you'll pick up what you need on the job.

Working Hours & Format
This role can be full-time or part-time, depending on your availability. It's remote, with occasional face-to-face working sessions. Our client operates on US business hours, so the schedule needs to align with US time zones — for engineers based in Ukraine, your working day will end around midnight Kyiv time.

Requirements
Solid fundamentals in C#, and a basic understanding of REST APIs.

Exposure to Azure is a plus, but not required — you'll pick up what you need on the job. We care more about your curiosity and attitude than your CV.
