# Go Developer

Gamzix

$$$$

Продуктова компанія

We are looking for an experienced Senior Go Developer to design, build, and scale our next-generation slot engine from the ground up under high-load conditions (10k+ RPS).

Responsibilities
- Design and develop a high-performance, scalable slot engine from scratch
- Ensure mathematical precision, RNG integration, and absolute data consistency
- Analyze and refactor existing Node.js modules, with potential migration to Go
- Optimize and maintain high-load services handling 10k+ requests per second
- Work with PostgreSQL partitioning, Redis state management, and messaging systems
- Collaborate directly with the CTO and core engineering team
- Contribute to architectural decisions and technical strategy

Requirements
- 5+ years of backend development experience
- Strong expertise in Go and deep understanding of concurrency and performance
- Experience working with high-load distributed systems
- Experience with PostgreSQL, including complex transactions and optimization
- Experience with Redis and message brokers (RabbitMQ or similar)
- Strong understanding of ACID principles and data consistency
- Experience working with legacy systems and refactoring is a strong advantage
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment

Nice to Have
- Domain experience in iGaming (slot engine mechanics, math models, RTP calculations, or payout logic).
- Prior experience executing tech stack migrations (e.g., Node.js to Go).
- Familiarity with ClickHouse, Docker, Kubernetes (K8s), and AWS infrastructure.

Tech Stack
- Backend: Go 1.20+
- Legacy/Migration: Node.js
- Storage & Cache: PostgreSQL (Partitioning, ACID), Redis, ClickHouse
- Infrastructure & Messaging: RabbitMQ, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS

What we offer
- Fully remote format
- Opportunity to build new game products from scratch
- Direct collaboration with product stakeholders and decision-makers
- Fast decision-making and startup-like environment
- A chance to influence the future portfolio of instant and crash games

Please note that feedback on your application will be provided within two weeks if a positive decision is made regarding your candidacy.
