About the Opportunity
WORKSHORE is looking for an experienced Senior iOS Software Engineer to join an international development team.
We are seeking a skilled iOS developer with strong expertise in Swift and SwiftUI, a solid understanding of mobile application architecture, and a commitment to building reliable, scalable, and maintainable applications.
The ideal candidate is proactive, detail-oriented, and comfortable working independently in a collaborative, English-speaking environment.

Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain high-quality native iOS applications using Swift and SwiftUI.
- Build reusable, maintainable, and testable application components.
- Implement new features and improve existing application functionality.
- Write and maintain unit tests to ensure code quality and reliability.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver robust mobile solutions.
- Participate in code reviews and technical discussions.
- Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks, bugs, and technical issues.
- Follow iOS development best practices and contribute to continuous improvements.

Requirements
- Strong commercial experience in native iOS development.
- Advanced proficiency in Swift.
- Hands-on experience developing applications with SwiftUI.
- Practical experience writing and maintaining Unit Tests.
- Solid understanding of iOS architecture patterns and software engineering principles.
- Experience working with REST APIs and integrating backend services.
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative development workflows.
- Ability to write clean, efficient, and maintainable code.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- English: Upper-Intermediate (B2+) or higher.
- Current location in Portugal, Spain, Latvia, or Lithuania.

Nice to Have
- Experience with UIKit and hybrid UIKit/SwiftUI applications.
- Knowledge of MVVM or other modern architectural patterns.
- Experience with Swift Concurrency (async/await).
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines for iOS applications.
- Experience with UI testing and automated testing frameworks.
- Understanding of mobile application performance optimization.

What We Offer
- Fully remote cooperation.
- Full-time engagement.
- Opportunity to work in an international engineering environment.
- Competitive hourly compensation of up to $22/hour.
- Opportunity to contribute to modern iOS applications and technical solutions.
