About the Role
We are looking for experienced OutSystems Developers to join a long-term project focused on building, maintaining, and optimizing enterprise web applications using the OutSystems low-code platform.
This opportunity is ideal for developers who have hands-on experience with OutSystems Reactive Web and Traditional Web Applications and enjoy creating scalable, high-performance business solutions.
You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business requirements into reliable, user-friendly applications and contribute to their continuous improvement.

Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain web applications using OutSystems.
- Build and enhance Reactive Web and Traditional Web Applications.
- Translate business requirements into technical solutions.
- Customize, optimize, and maintain existing OutSystems applications.
- Integrate applications with external systems using REST/SOAP APIs.
- Ensure application performance, scalability, security, and maintainability.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and improve existing functionality.
- Collaborate with developers, business analysts, and other stakeholders.
- Follow OutSystems development best practices and coding standards.

Requirements
- 2+ years of commercial experience with OutSystems.
- Hands-on experience developing OutSystems web applications.
- Practical knowledge of Reactive Web and/or Traditional Web Applications.
- Experience integrating external services using REST/SOAP APIs.
- Understanding of web application architecture and development principles.
- Ability to analyze business requirements and implement technical solutions.
- Experience maintaining, customizing, and optimizing existing applications.
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a distributed team.

Nice to Have
- OutSystems certifications (Associate / Professional / Expert).
- Experience with JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.
- Knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Experience with enterprise applications and business process automation.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies.
- Experience with application performance optimization.

What We Offer
- Long-term, full-time collaboration.
- Remote work opportunities.
- Opportunity to work with the OutSystems low-code ecosystem.
- International project environment.
- Challenging enterprise application development tasks.
- Competitive compensation based on experience.
