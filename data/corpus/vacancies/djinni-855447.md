# Senior Data Engineer (Python / Airflow / dbt / GCP)

PulseRise Technologies

$$$$

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to own the data platform of a fast-growing startup, from architecture all the way to production. The company helps investors and other teams find and research companies, so reliable, fresh and well-structured data is at the heart of the product. Today the platform processes data one job at a time in containers. You will lead its move to running many jobs at once, with event-driven data intake and orchestrated batch workflows. Your daily work will combine Python, Airflow, dbt, GCP and PostgreSQL with large-scale web scraping from many sources. You will also solve hard data problems, such as matching company and people records across sources and using LLMs to pull structured data out of text. This role suits someone who likes building from zero, enjoys owning a wide area, works well on their own and is comfortable in a fully remote team with no fixed working hours.

## Details

- Schedule: Full time
- Location: Remote – EU
- Start: ASAP
- Duration: Long-term
- English: Fluent
- Type of collaboration: B2B Contract

## About the project

The client is a startup building a data platform that helps investors and other teams find and research companies. The platform collects data from many sources, cleans it, links related records and turns it into useful insights about companies. As the product grows, the current one-at-a-time processing needs to become a faster, event-driven system that handles many jobs at once. The Senior Data Engineer will lead this change and own the whole platform, from design to day-to-day running. The team is small, so engineers own a wide area and have a direct impact on the product. The company works fully remotely, and you don't need to overlap with anyone's working hours.

## You have

- Experience building a production data platform with many data sources from zero, including its architecture, deployment and day-to-day running
- Production experience with workflow orchestration tools (Airflow, Dagster, Prefect or Temporal)
- Hands-on experience with dbt and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- Strong Python skills, including asynchronous HTTP and concurrent processing
- Hands-on web scraping experience (Scrapy, Playwright, Selenium or Beautiful Soup)
- Strong PostgreSQL skills: data modeling, query optimization and reliable incremental loading
- Experience with event-driven architecture, including retries, failure recovery and dead-letter queues
- Comfort working fully remotely and on your own, with no fixed hours that overlap with the team
- Fluent English

## Nice to have

- Experience in startups or small engineering teams where you owned a wide area
- Entity resolution experience, especially matching and reconciling company and people records across sources
- Experience using LLMs to extract structured data or enrich data
- Familiarity with BigQuery and Dataform
- Background in company, investment or B2B data platforms

## What to do

- Own the data platform from start to finish, from architecture to production and day-to-day running
- Lead the move from one-at-a-time container processing to concurrent, event-driven data intake and orchestrated batch workflows
- Build and maintain data pipelines with Python, Airflow, dbt and GCP
- Build and scale web scraping that runs across many sources at once, handling rate limits and failures
- Design PostgreSQL data models, optimize queries, and set up reliable incremental loads, idempotent upserts and backfills of historical data
- Build solid failure recovery, retries and dead-letter handling for event-driven data intake
- Match and reconcile company and people records across different data sources
- Use LLMs to extract and enrich structured data where it helps
