About the role
You will join the cloud infrastructure team responsible for the platform everything else runs on. This is not a "keep the lights on" role: the goal is to turn a large, fast-growing stateful system into a predictable, well-automated platform. Scale: petabytes of data, thousands of cores, constant ingestion, multi-region and multi-account AWS.

What you will do
- Operate EKS clusters across several environments (Karpenter, Cilium, ArgoCD / GitOps)
- Manage a multi-account AWS organization: provisioning, networking, IAM, cross-account connectivity
- Maintain and evolve the Terraform / Terragrunt IaC platform and its CI pipelines
- Improve tooling for deploys, schema changes, backups, restores and incident response
- Remove repeat operational pain through automation and self-healing systems
- Keep cloud spend under control
- Take part in on-call, with a focus on making incidents rarer

Requirements
- Deep production experience with Kubernetes (EKS preferred) at large scale
- Strong experience running production infrastructure on AWS across multiple accounts (IAM, networking)
- Terraform or Terragrunt at scale, including module design and state management
- Solid Linux knowledge (disk, memory, networking, failure modes) - Experience supporting stateful systems (databases, queues, storage)
- Comfortable owning systems end-to-end, including on-call - Fluent English

Nice to have
- ArgoCD and GitHub Actions
- Multi-region infrastructure
- Building infrastructure services for AI agents
