# Senior Blockchain / DLT Engineer

MeGaDev

$$$$

Core Domain: Digital Asset Platforms & Tokenization
Project Context
We are looking for an experienced Blockchain / DLT Engineer to support the design, development, integration, and deployment of enterprise digital asset platforms and asset tokenization solutions.
The role will focus on translating high-level architecture and business requirements into reliable, secure, and production-ready implementations for financial institutions and enterprise clients.
Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, test, and deploy smart contracts supporting digital asset issuance, tokenization, transfers, and lifecycle management.
- Implement blockchain-based business logic and asset management workflows.
- Develop integrations between DLT networks, backend services, APIs, and frontend applications.
- Build and maintain transaction processing components, including asynchronous operations, event handling, and transaction state management.
- Implement automated testing, including unit, integration, and fuzz testing.
- Support blockchain infrastructure configuration, deployment automation, and CI/CD processes.
- Collaborate with solution architects, cloud engineers, and business stakeholders.
- Contribute to technical design decisions and troubleshoot complex DLT integration challenges.
- Support proof-of-concept, pilot, and production implementation activities.
Required Technical Skills
- Strong hands-on experience with Solidity and EVM-compatible blockchain platforms.
- Practical experience developing and deploying smart contracts.
- Understanding of tokenization architectures and digital asset lifecycle management.
- Experience integrating blockchain applications with backend systems and APIs.
- Understanding of asynchronous processing, blockchain events, transaction finality, and failure handling.
- Experience with smart contract testing frameworks, automated testing, and security best practices.
- Familiarity with cloud environments, deployment pipelines, and version control.
- Ability to work independently from architectural specifications.
Nice to Have
- Experience with permissioned or enterprise DLT networks.
- Experience with financial services, capital markets, or institutional digital assets.
- Knowledge of custody integrations and transaction signing.
- Familiarity with token standards and access-control mechanisms.
- Experience with Rust or non-EVM blockchain ecosystems.
- Polish language proficiency.
