About the Role
We are looking for an experienced Principal Front-End Developer / Staff Engineer (React) to lead the technical development of modern, scalable, AI-powered web applications.
This is a hands-on technical leadership role that combines advanced React engineering, front-end architecture, and the integration of AI/LLM capabilities into production applications.
You will take ownership of critical architectural decisions, establish engineering best practices, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality solutions in a fast-paced product environment.
The ideal candidate is a senior technical expert who can balance strategic architectural thinking with hands-on development and contribute effectively from day one.
Key Responsibilities

Front-End Architecture & Development
- Design and implement scalable, maintainable front-end architectures using React and TypeScript.
- Develop high-performance, responsive web applications using modern JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, and Tailwind CSS.
- Build reusable components, shared libraries, and design systems.
- Define and implement effective state management strategies.
- Optimize application performance, rendering efficiency, and user experience.
- Ensure code quality, accessibility, maintainability, and test coverage.

AI Integration & Product Development
- Integrate AI/LLM APIs into front-end applications.
- Develop intuitive interfaces for AI-powered features and agentic workflows.
- Collaborate with backend and AI engineers to integrate intelligent services into production applications.
- Design user experiences for asynchronous AI interactions, streaming responses, and complex workflows.
- Ensure reliable communication between front-end applications and AI-powered backend services.

Technical Leadership
- Drive front-end architectural decisions and establish technical standards.
- Conduct code reviews and mentor developers.
- Identify and resolve complex technical challenges.
- Improve development workflows, engineering practices, and application scalability.
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and backend engineers.
- Take ownership of technical delivery and contribute to product-level decisions.

Requirements
- 10+ years of professional front-end development experience.
- Extensive commercial experience with React and TypeScript.
- Previous experience as a Lead Front-End Developer, Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer, or in a comparable technical leadership role.
- Expert knowledge of JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, and CSS3.
- Strong hands-on experience with Tailwind CSS.
- Deep understanding of front-end architecture and scalable application design.
- Proven experience building reusable components and design systems.
- Strong knowledge of state management patterns and libraries.
- Experience with front-end performance optimization, testing, and debugging.
- Hands-on experience integrating AI/LLM APIs or agentic workflows into production applications.
- Experience working in Agile product teams or startup environments.
- Strong problem-solving skills, technical ownership, and mentoring capabilities.
- English B2 or higher.

Nice to Have
- Experience with Agentic AI and AI-powered product interfaces.
- Knowledge of Go or Python.
- Experience with AWS services, particularly Lambda, S3, and CloudFront.
- Experience with real-time applications, streaming APIs, or asynchronous workflows.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and cloud deployment practices.
- Experience building products from early-stage development through production.

What We Offer
- Fully remote opportunity for candidates based in Ukraine.
- Full-time engagement for 3 months.
- Opportunity to work on modern AI-powered applications.
- Significant technical ownership and architectural influence.
- Collaboration with experienced engineering and product teams.
- Fast-paced environment with challenging technical tasks.
- Immediate start.
