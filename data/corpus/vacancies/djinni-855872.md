Python AI Product Engineer (FastAPI / LLM)

Location: Remote (Europe / Ukraine)
Employment: Full-time
Company: MTicket

About Us
MTicket is a technology company operating in the ticketing and live events industry. Our platforms support ticket sales, event management, payments, marketing, customer interactions, and various business operations.
We are actively developing our AI engineering direction to introduce intelligent automation and AI-powered functionality into our products and internal processes.
We're looking for a Senior Python AI Product Engineer who can turn ideas and business challenges into real, production-ready AI solutions.
This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who enjoys building products, experimenting with modern AI capabilities, and delivering working solutions.
We're not looking for someone who simply uses AI coding assistants. We need an engineer who has already built and launched AI-powered applications using LLM APIs.

What You'll Do

- Design, develop, and launch AI-powered applications and product features using Python and FastAPI.
- Integrate LLMs such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar providers through APIs.
- Build intelligent assistants, AI agents, and automated workflows to solve real business problems.
- Develop backend services that connect AI capabilities with existing business systems.
- Implement LLM tool/function calling, structured outputs, and multi-step workflows.
- Integrate third-party APIs, databases, and internal services.
- Work on AI solutions for ticketing, customer support, marketing, analytics, and operational processes.
- Collaborate with other engineers to build scalable and reliable AI-powered functionality.
- Take ownership of features from technical design and implementation to deployment and production support.
- Continuously improve AI functionality, response quality, performance, and API usage costs.

Our Tech Stack

- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- AI: LLM API integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar)
- Architecture: REST APIs, backend services, third-party integrations
- Development: Git

Our approach is pragmatic: we choose technologies based on the problem we're solving rather than relying on a specific AI framework.

What We're Looking For

- 3+ years of professional Python backend development experience.
- Solid commercial experience with FastAPI and backend API development.
- Proven experience building and launching AI-powered applications or product features in production.
- Hands-on experience integrating LLMs through APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or similar).
- Experience developing AI assistants, AI agents, intelligent automation, or multi-step LLM workflows.
- Strong understanding of REST APIs, integrations, databases, and backend architecture.
- Experience implementing and maintaining production applications.
- Ability to independently design and deliver complete features.
- Understanding of AI application limitations, error handling, response validation, and API cost management.
- Strong problem-solving skills, ownership, and a product-oriented mindset.
- Ukrainian or Russian language proficiency at C1 level or higher.

Nice to Have

- Experience developing AI-first SaaS products or commercial AI applications.
- Hands-on experience with LangChain, LangGraph, or similar AI orchestration frameworks.
- Experience with RAG, embeddings, vector databases, or MCP.
- Experience building AI workflows involving multiple tools or external services.
- Familiarity with LLM evaluation, prompt optimization, and structured output validation.
- Experience in e-commerce, marketplaces, payments, ticketing, or marketing automation.
- Experience with cloud infrastructure, Docker, and asynchronous backend processing.
- Experience independently shipping products from idea to production.

What Makes a Great Candidate

We especially value engineers who have already built real AI applications and can confidently explain:

- What AI product or functionality they developed.
- What problem it solved and who used it.
- How LLM APIs were integrated into the application.
- What backend architecture and integrations they implemented.
- Which parts of the solution they personally owned.
- How they handled errors, unexpected AI responses, and production challenges.
- How the product was deployed and improved based on real-world usage.

We value practical engineering experience, independent thinking, and the ability to deliver useful products over knowledge of specific frameworks.

What We Offer

- The opportunity to build AI-powered products for an established ticketing and live events business.
- A growing AI engineering direction with significant room for innovation.
- Real business challenges where your work can have a measurable impact.
- Ownership of AI functionality from idea to production.
- Direct collaboration with engineering and business teams.
- Freedom to propose technical solutions and experiment with modern AI technologies.
- A fully remote working environment.
- A collaborative team and a practical, results-oriented engineering culture.

Interested?

If you've already built AI-powered products using Python and LLM APIs, enjoy solving real business problems, and want to see your solutions used in production, we'd love to hear from you.
When applying, please briefly describe an AI-powered product or feature you've personally built and launched. Feel free to share relevant GitHub repositories, product links, or examples of your work.
